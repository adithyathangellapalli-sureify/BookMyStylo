 01 · Foundations 
 The design system
 One teal palette, a Georgia serif for headlines paired with the system sans, and a set of component primitives shared by all three apps. Colour is tokenised, so every screen below re-themes from these same variables.
 Palette
 Neutrals lean teal rather than grey. Semantic colours (available / booked / held / leave / closed) encode booking & slot state and are separate from the brand accent. Toggle the theme (top-right) to see every token shift.

 How they compare
 Option
 Best when…
 Trade-off
 A · Stylist tabs 
 The customer has a preferred stylist
 One extra tap if they don't care who
 B · Time of day 
 Time matters more than the person

 Many stylists & slots; a “board” feel
 Horizontal scroll can hide later times

 How it works (for review)
 Customer. Taps Pay online → sees the QR / Open UPI app → the sheet enters a “Waiting for the saloon to confirm…” state that polls every few seconds. The old “I've paid” button is gone.
 The UPI link is pre-filled. Open UPI app / the QR carry the shop's VPA , payee name and amount (and a booking-ref note so the shop can match it), so the customer just enters their UPI PIN. On a personal VPA the amount can technically be edited, which is why the shop verifies it.
 Saloon confirms. The booking shows 💳 Paying online with Confirm ₹300 received — the shop checks their UPI app, verifies the amount, and taps it. That's the source of truth, so the customer can't fake it. “Not received yet” keeps the customer waiting and changes nothing.
 Auto success. The customer's next poll sees PAID → the screen flips to Payment successful on its own (also reflected in My bookings). The customer never self-confirms.
 Stuck / slow. After a short wait the customer sees a time-based “taking longer” nudge (never triggered by the shop, so no false failures) with Message the saloon , Show QR again , and Pay cash instead . Closing the screen is safe — it still confirms in the background.
 Two-step confirm (A → step → A). From A , tapping either button opens a confirm step; Cancel returns to A unchanged, so no single tap ever commits. Proceeding on “Confirm” marks it Paid (state D), which still offers Undo → A for ~10 min in case it was wrong (the customer's receipt flips back with a “reopened — don't pay again” note). Past the window or after the service, the existing Refund handles money that truly needs returning.
 Later, a real PSP (Razorpay/Cashfree) can replace the shop-confirm step with an automatic webhook — same screens, the “Confirm” tap just becomes automatic. The flow is built so that swap is additive.
